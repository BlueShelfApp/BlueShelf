BlueShelf

Privacy

Last updated 29 September 2026

The app collects nothing. BlueShelf running on your PC has no account, makes no internet connection of its own and sends no telemetry. It never sees what is in your library, and neither does anyone else. Everything it works out about your videos stays in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BlueShelf on your own machine, and your video files are never modified or moved.

This website

These are static pages. There is no account, no sign-up, no comments and no newsletter, so there is nothing to give us and nothing of yours to store.

There are also no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

What the server records

The web server keeps ordinary access logs — the page requested, the time, your IP address and your browser's user-agent string. That is standard for any web server and is used only to investigate faults and abuse. Logs are kept briefly and then deleted.

Fonts

The pages load a typeface from Google Fonts, which means Google receives the request and your IP address when a page loads. That is the only third party involved in this site.

Downloads

The download button sends you to GitHub, which is a separate service with its own privacy policy. Nothing about your visit here is passed to them beyond the ordinary act of following a link.

Contact

Questions about any of this, including anything you would like removed, go to BlueShelfApp@proton.me.
